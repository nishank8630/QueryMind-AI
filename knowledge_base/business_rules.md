# Centralized Business Metric Definitions and Accounting Rules

## 1. Gross Merchandise Value (GMV)
- **Definition**: The total value of merchandise ordered across all orders before subtracting discounts, returns, or cancellations, including shipping cost.
- **Formula**: `SUM(payments.amount)` for all created orders, OR `SUM((order_items.quantity * order_items.unit_price) + orders.shipping_cost)`.
- **Primary Source**: `orders`, `order_items`.
- **Note**: GMV measures top-line marketplace throughput, regardless of final completion status.

---

## 2. Gross Sales Revenue
- **Definition**: Total product sale value before item discounts, shipping, or returns for valid (non-cancelled) orders.
- **Formula**: `SUM(order_items.quantity * order_items.unit_price)` WHERE `orders.order_status != 'Cancelled'`.
- **Tables Used**: `orders`, `order_items`.

---

## 3. Net Sales Revenue (Primary Revenue Metric)
- **Definition**: Actual business revenue earned from completed orders after subtracting item discounts and customer refund amounts.
- **Formula**:
  `Gross Product Revenue` - `Item Discounts` - `Eligible Refunds`.
- **SQL Implementation**:
  ```sql
  WITH gross_rev AS (
      SELECT 
          o.order_id,
          SUM(oi.quantity * (oi.unit_price - oi.item_discount)) AS net_items
      FROM orders o
      JOIN order_items oi ON o.order_id = oi.order_id
      WHERE o.order_status = 'Completed'
      GROUP BY o.order_id
  ),
  refunds_agg AS (
      SELECT 
          order_id, 
          SUM(refund_amount) AS total_refunded
      FROM returns
      WHERE return_status = 'Approved'
      GROUP BY order_id
  )
  SELECT 
      COALESCE(SUM(g.net_items - COALESCE(r.total_refunded, 0)), 0) AS net_revenue
  FROM gross_rev g
  LEFT JOIN refunds_agg r ON g.order_id = r.order_id;
  ```
- **Crucial Rule**: Never perform direct joins between `order_items`, `payments`, and `returns` without aggregating subqueries, otherwise a cartesian product occurs and revenue is multiplied.

---

## 4. Average Order Value (AOV)
- **Definition**: Average amount spent per completed order.
- **Formula**: `SUM(payments.amount) / COUNT(DISTINCT orders.order_id)` WHERE `orders.order_status = 'Completed' AND payments.payment_status = 'Success'`.

---

## 5. Refunds & Return Rate
- **Definition**: 
  - Total Refunds: `SUM(refund_amount)` WHERE `return_status = 'Approved'`.
  - Return Rate (by Product): `(SUM(quantity_returned)::float / NULLIF(SUM(order_items.quantity), 0)) * 100`.
- **Tables Used**: `returns`, `order_items`.

---

## 6. Customer Repeat Purchase Rate
- **Definition**: Percentage of paying customers who have placed 2 or more orders.
- **Formula**: `(COUNT(DISTINCT repeat_customers)::float / NULLIF(COUNT(DISTINCT all_ordering_customers), 0)) * 100`.
- **Tables Used**: `orders`.

---

## 7. Website Conversion Rate
- **Definition**: Percentage of website visits that resulted in a completed conversion/transaction.
- **Formula**: `(SUM(CASE WHEN converted = true THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)::float / NULLIF(COUNT(*), 0)) * 100`.
- **Tables Used**: `website_sessions`.

---

## 8. Product Profitability
- **Definition**: Total gross profit generated by a product or category.
- **Formula**: `SUM(oi.quantity * (oi.unit_price - oi.item_discount - p.unit_cost))` WHERE `orders.order_status = 'Completed'`.
- **Tables Used**: `order_items`, `products`, `orders`.

---

## 9. Handling Cancelled Orders
- Cancelled orders (`order_status = 'Cancelled'`) must **ALWAYS** be excluded from net revenue, AOV, and customer lifetime spending calculations.
- Payments associated with cancelled orders have `payment_status = 'Failed'` or `'Refunded'`.
